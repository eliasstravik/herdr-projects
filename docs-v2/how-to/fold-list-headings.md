# Fold list headings

This page tells you how to fold and unfold the headings of the task list, so that you see only the tasks you need.
For how headings look and behave, see [list headings](../reference/list-headings.md).

## Group the list

**Note:** `Alt+M` goes through three views: this machine only, every machine, then each machine alone.

1. To change between the grouping by repo and the grouping by status, press `Alt+S`.
2. To show the tasks of every machine, press `Alt+M` until the board shows every machine. Each machine then has its
   own heading.

## Fold a heading

1. Move the selection to the heading with `↑` or `↓`. The arrow keys stop on every heading.
2. Look at the hint line. It shows what `Enter` will do, for example `enter fold this repo`.
3. Press `Enter`. The tasks of the heading hide, and the heading shows how many tasks it holds.

As an alternative, click the heading.

**Note:** When you fold a machine heading, all its repos and tasks hide. When you unfold it, a repo that you folded
before stays folded.

## Unfold a heading

1. Move the selection to the folded heading (`▸`).
2. Press `Enter`, or click the heading.

**Note:** A machine with no task has a folded heading that cannot unfold. `Enter` says so. The first task of the
machine opens the heading.

## See tasks under a folded heading

You do not have to unfold a heading to see a matching task.

1. Press `Ctrl+F`.
2. Type the words or the number of the task. Every task that matches shows, folded or not.
3. To clear the filter, press `Esc`. The folds come back.

## Fold a heading on the web board

1. Tap the heading of a machine or of a group.
2. To unfold it, tap it again.

**Note:** On a watching device, a fold changes only what that page shows.
