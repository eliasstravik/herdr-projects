# Why titles are short

This page tells why a task title has a limit of 60 characters, and why the board shortens a long title and does not
refuse it. For the rules, see [titles](../reference/titles.md).

## Why 60

60 characters is about the space that a title gets on a list row. In a window of 120 columns with the task panel
open, the list is 72 cells wide. The mark and the time use the other cells.

60 characters is also the space that Herdr's sidebar gives to the name of the task's space, after the task number.
Thus you can read a 60-character title fully in both places. Before the limit, a title of 100 characters filled the
row and pushed out everything else.

## Why the board shortens and does not refuse

When an agent adds a task for you, the task is never lost because of its title. The answer of the command tells you
what to do next: rename the task if a better short title exists. The board keeps the full text in the description, so
no words are lost.

## Why the board counts cells

The board counts characters by their space on the screen, because that is how it draws rows. Thus a title at the limit
fills the same space in every language.
